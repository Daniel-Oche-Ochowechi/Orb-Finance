CORE ARCHITECTURE

Our principles

The mathematical and regulatory foundations governing every settlement.

01

Designed for Nigeria. Built for the world.

We begin where the need is greatest, then extend outward over the same network. Deep local clearing rails coupled directly to global liquidity hubs.

02

Exact by design.

Every movement of value is recorded in an immutable double-entry ledger and reconciled daily. The numbers always agree with cryptographic precision.

03

Compliance at the core.

Sanctions screening, PEP verification, and risk scoring occur before funds move, never after. Regulatory compliance as a first-order engineering principle.

04

Intelligence that grows with the network.

As volume builds, Orb applies increasingly capable fraud detection, adaptive routing, dynamic pricing and institutional liquidity planning.
