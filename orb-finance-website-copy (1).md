## The shift

**Money should move like information.**

Today, sending money to another country means layers of intermediaries, each adding delay and cost, and often an unclear amount arriving at the other end. Your teams spend their time connecting what should already be connected.

With Orb, you send in your currency. They receive in theirs. The rate is shown before you commit.

---

## The platform

**One integration. Three capabilities.**

**Cross-border transfers**
Send your local currency to a person in another country. They receive the equivalent amount in their own local currency, at a rate and fee you can see before you confirm.

**Domestic transfers**
Precise, dependable movement of Naira within Nigeria, with a clear status at every step.

**Bill payments**
Electricity, airtime, data and television, available inside your own product.

---

## How it works

**01. Connect**
Integrate once. Rehearse the entire flow in our sandbox before anything goes live.

**02. Move**
Request a quote in the sender's currency, see exactly what the recipient will receive in theirs, and confirm. Orb handles screening, conversion and settlement.

**03. Observe**
Watch every transaction in real time. Receive a signed webhook the moment a status changes.

## Principles

**Designed for Nigeria. Built for the world.**
We begin where the need is greatest, then extend outward over the same network.

**Exact by design.**
Every movement of value is recorded in a double-entry ledger and reconciled daily. The numbers always agree.

**Compliance at the core.**
Sanctions and risk screening occur before funds move, never after.

**Intelligence that grows with the network.**
As volume builds, Orb applies increasingly capable fraud detection, pricing and liquidity planning.

## FAQ

**When does Orb Finance launch?**
On 15 January 2027. Early access members hear from us first.

**How does sending money work?**
You send in your local currency, for example Naira. The recipient, in another country, receives the equivalent amount in their local currency. The exchange rate and any fee are shown before you confirm.

**Who is Orb for?**
Banks, fintechs and businesses that want to send money across borders, move money at home and pay bills through one integration. Individuals may join the list too, and we will tell you when personal access opens.

**Does it cost anything to join?**
No. Early access is free and carries no obligation.

**What happens after I sign up?**
You will receive a confirmation, then updates as launch approaches, then an invitation when your access opens.

**Can we connect before launch?**
Join as a bank or fintech, or request sandbox access, and our team will contact you.
